# Generation prompts

Mode: built-in `image_gen.imagegen`. These PNGs are generated raster assets; no CLI fallback was used.

## App icon

Use case: logo-brand. Production app icon for Tempo, a personal day planner that turns one voice or text brain dump into a clear daily schedule. One finished square icon, not a board or mockup. Full-bleed deep navy #17202A background; no baked-in rounded corners. Center an original lowercase t-like continuous coral #E86A4A ribbon, horizontal stroke suggesting a morning horizon, vertical stroke turning into a check-like forward path. A mint #B9E6D3 circular sun floats at the upper right. Generous padding, clear at 24 pixels, calm and precise. No words, calendar grid, clock hands, sparkle, border, or watermark.

## Standalone symbol

Edit the app icon into a standalone transparent symbol. Preserve the coral t/path and mint sun proportions, remove the navy background to true alpha, center in a square with clear padding. Cleanup pass: keep the exact symbol geometry, remove stray flecks, edge noise, white fringes and grunge; retain only the coral path and mint disk on transparency.

## Final horizontal logo

Create the official horizontal Tempo logo on a solid warm off-white #F5F5F0 background. Use the app icon as the identity reference: coral t-like rounded path and mint sun, cleanly recreated to the left. Set the exact lowercase word “tempo” to the right in dark navy #17202A, confident rounded geometric sans-serif, medium-bold. A single centered logo lockup with generous breathing space. No stains, stray marks, textures, gradients, yellow, other words, mockups, or presentation boards. Wide 3:1 canvas, opaque light background to the image edges.

The final assets in this folder are the selected outputs. Earlier transparent wordmark experiments were discarded.
